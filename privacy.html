🎨 Paint by Numbers — Privacy Policy

Data collected: none

Last updated: 2026

Paint by Numbers is a painting game for children. It is designed to be completely private and safe.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party software development kits.

What stays on the device

Your child's painting progress is saved only on your own device (in the browser or app's local storage) so a picture can be resumed later. This never leaves the device and is not visible to us or anyone else. Removing the app clears it.

Network

The game works fully offline. It does not require an internet connection to play and does not send any data anywhere.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it complies with children's privacy regulations such as COPPA and GDPR-K by design. There is nothing to collect, profile, or advertise against.

Open source

The full source code is public, so anyone can verify these claims: github.com/maximstark/paint-by-numbers.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the GitHub repository linked above.
